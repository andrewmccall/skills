# Voice pass

Protect identity rather than merely improving prose.

Load `VOICE.md`, `PROFILE.md` and relevant approved examples from the user-named profile or the first available profile at `.writing/`, `.writing-profile/`, `~/.writing/` or `~/.codex/writing-profile/`. If no voice profile exists, ask for a sample or explain that only a generic voice review is possible; do not pretend to recognise a voice you have not been shown.

## Diagnose

Identify passages that:

- sound unlike the profile;
- could have appeared in any competent document on the topic;
- introduce a model's house style, rhetorical habits or vocabulary;
- smooth away useful uncertainty, roughness or personality;
- imitate surface quirks while missing the profile's way of reasoning;
- violate a format constraint supplied by the user.

Approved examples illustrate the written guide; they do not create secret rules. Current user instructions outrank the profile.

## Revise carefully

Prefer local revisions. Preserve meaning, factual confidence, quotations and technical precision. Do not manufacture biography, experience, jokes or favourite phrases to simulate authenticity.

When the profile itself conflicts or lacks evidence, name the ambiguity rather than averaging it into bland prose.

Return either a focused diagnostic with proposed changes or the revised text, depending on the user's request. Mention any phrase intentionally left rough because it carries the voice.
